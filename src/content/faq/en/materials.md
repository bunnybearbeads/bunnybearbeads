<p>
  I exclusively use the finest Japanese glass beads from the known universe, combining Toho and Miyuki. My favorite is Toho Aiko for its mathematically precise calibration, which I pair with Miyuki Delica for their unbeatable color palette.
</p>
<p>
  For threads, it's almost always Fireline — a virtually bulletproof combination that never stretches or frays over time.
</p>
