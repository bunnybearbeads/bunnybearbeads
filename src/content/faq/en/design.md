<p>
  Designing complex beadwork charts from scratch is a craft in itself that I am still practicing.
</p>
<p>
  Thankfully, the craft world has thousands of incredible ready-made patterns created by indie artisans and pattern makers (on Etsy and beyond). I select the most charming ones, craft them with top-tier materials, and always credit the original pattern creators.
</p>
