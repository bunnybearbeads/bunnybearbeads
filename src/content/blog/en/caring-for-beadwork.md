<p>
  Handmade beaded jewelry is delicate yet surprisingly durable when treated with kindness. Keep it away from perfume, store flat in a velvet pouch, and avoid direct soaking in chlorinated water. When cleaning, gently wipe with a dry microfiber cloth.
</p>
