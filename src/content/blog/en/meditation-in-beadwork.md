<p>
  In our fast-paced screen-dominated existence, working with miniature physical objects grounds us in the present moment. Counting beads, feeling the tactile tension of silk thread, and watching geometry emerge row by row creates a flow state akin to deep breath meditation. Many collectors say that merely wearing such handmade pieces reminds them to pause and breathe throughout the busy day.
